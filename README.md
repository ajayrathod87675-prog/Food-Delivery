# 🍔 Food Delivery Data Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing food delivery data to understand sales performance, customer ordering patterns, popular food items, and payment preferences.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

* Analyze total sales by city.
* Identify the top 10 food items by total sales.
* Analyze sales by payment method.
* Clean and prepare data for analysis.
* Generate meaningful business insights.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

* **Excel:** Data inspection and cleaning.
* **Python:** Data analysis.
* **Pandas:** Data cleaning, grouping, filtering, and aggregation.
* **Matplotlib:** Data visualization.
* **Power BI:** Interactive dashboard and KPI visualization.

## 🔄 Project Workflow

1. Data collection
2. Data cleaning and preprocessing
3. Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
4. Sales calculation and aggregation
5. Data visualization
6. Dashboard creation
7. Business insights and recommendations

## 📊 Key Analysis

* City-wise total sales
* Food item-wise total sales
* Top 10 food items by sales
* Payment method-wise sales
* Sales performance visualization

## 💡 Business Value

The analysis can help food delivery businesses understand which cities and food items generate the most sales and which payment methods customers use most frequently.

## 🚀 Future Scope

* Analyze monthly and seasonal sales trends.
* Study customer retention and ordering frequency.
* Track delivery time and cancellation rates.
* Build an interactive dashboard with additional KPIs.
* Develop sales forecasting using machine learning.
